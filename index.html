FILE 02 / MANIFESTO

    
Stores don't grow by accident.
They grow by architecture.

    

      
For ten years I've done both jobs that usually fight each other: the operator chasing revenue and the engineer chasing clean systems. That tension is the whole craft — a storefront is not a website, it's a machine that turns attention into orders, and every part either compounds or leaks.

      
I build Shopify systems the way an architect builds load-bearing walls: theme engineering that survives updates, apps that earn their keep, SEO wired into the structure instead of painted on, and growth loops measured in weeks, not vibes. If it can't be measured, it doesn't ship.

      
Based in Hong Kong. Working globally. The mission is simple: give small brands the infrastructure of big ones — which is why my entire merchant toolset is free.
